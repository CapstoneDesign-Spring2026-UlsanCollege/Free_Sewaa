# Final MVP Demo Guide

## Demonstration Flow

1. Open the landing page and explain the donation problem.
2. Create or access a non-sensitive demonstration account.
3. Browse and filter donation items.
4. Post a donation with representative data.
5. Open an item and submit a request.
6. Show messaging or explain the linked communication flow.
7. Show user activity and administration views.
8. Close with tests, limitations, and next work.

## Environments

- Primary: [Render](https://free-sewaa-qh05.onrender.com)
- Fallback: [Vercel](https://free-sewaa.vercel.app)
- Local: follow the [Setup and Run Guide](./SETUP_AND_RUN_GUIDE.md)

Do not publish reusable demo passwords. Prepare accounts before the presentation.

## Honest Failure Handling

If a live step fails, state what failed, switch to a verified environment or saved evidence, and explain the expected request/data flow. Do not describe an unverified step as successful.

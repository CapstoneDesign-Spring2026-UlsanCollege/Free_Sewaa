# User Guide — Free Sewaa

Free Sewaa is a community donation platform. You can give away things you no longer need and find items that others donated.

---

## Getting Started

### Sign Up

1. Go to https://free-sewaa-qh05.onrender.com
2. Click Sign Up in the top menu
3. Fill in your first name, last name, email, and password
4. Password must be 8–10 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and a number
5. Use a real email address (@gmail.com, @naver.com, @daum.net, etc.)
6. Click submit and you will be logged in automatically

### Sign In

1. Click Sign In
2. Enter the email and password you used to sign up
3. Click submit

---

## Browsing Items

After logging in, you will see the browse page with items people donated.

- Scroll through the grid to see all items
- Use the search bar to find specific items
- Use the category filter to narrow down (Clothing, Books, Electronics, etc.)
- Click on any item card to see full details

---

## Donating an Item

To give away something:

1. Click Donate in the top menu
2. Fill in:
   - Title (e.g., "Winter coat, size M")
   - Description (condition, pickup location, etc.)
   - Category (choose the best fit)
   - Image URL (optional — a default image will be used)
3. Click Submit
4. Your item will appear in the browse page

---

## Requesting an Item

When you find something you need:

1. Click on the item to open details
2. Click Request or Contact Donor
3. You will see the donor's information
4. Send them a message to arrange pickup

---

## Messaging

1. Click Messages in the top menu
2. You will see your conversations
3. Click a conversation to read messages
4. Type your reply and send it

---

## My Items and Requests

Click on your profile or dashboard to see:

- Items you donated
- Items you requested
- Status of your requests (pending, approved, completed)

---

## Admin Panel

Only admin users can access this.

1. Go to https://free-sewaa-qh05.onrender.com/admin_login.html
2. Sign in with admin credentials
3. View platform overview: total users, items, messages
4. Manage users and listings

---

## Tips

- Always use a real email address (demo emails like @demo.com are blocked)
- If you get an error, check that all fields are filled in correctly
- Log out when done on a shared computer
